Orders — live stream

A background producer invents a random order every ~0.5–1.5 s, appends it to the event stream, and replays it through the versioned handlers into the OrderSummary projection. About one in four events is a loyalty bonus applied with an Update: it lands on an existing order, or is a clean no-op when it targets an order that was never placed — watch the feed, and note that no ORD-GHOST-* row ever appears in the table.
